DESIGN SYSTEM v1.0
EmilioSierra.com — Creative Technology Studio
Design thesis
Near-future Miami expressed through editorial sophistication, modern technology and human personality.

Not sci-fi. Not retro Miami. Not a generic developer portfolio.
The website should feel like Emilio's digital storefront in a Miami that is a few years ahead of today.

04 / SHAPE LANGUAGE
Architectural.
Mostly rectangular.
Minimal rounding.
Working radii:
04px / SMALL
08px / MEDIUM
16px / LARGE
Large photography can have 0px radius.
Pills are reserved for controls where their shape actually serves a purpose.
No portfolio made out of twenty floating rounded cards.
No gratuitous glass panels.
No enormous shadows.
Depth comes from composition, photography, light and movement.

07 / MOTION
Our rule is officially locked:
LARGE THINGS MOVE SLOWLY.
SMALL THINGS RESPOND QUICKLY.

Four motion families:
FAST / ~160MS
Microinteraction.
NORMAL / ~260MS
Interface response.
SLOW / ~500MS
Editorial transitions.
CINEMATIC / ~800MS
Major environmental moments.
These are starting values rather than requirements to animate everything.
Motion communicates:
Hierarchy.
Continuity.
State.
Cause and effect.
Never:
"Look how many animations I know."
And reduced-motion users get the same design quality, not a stripped-down second-class version.

11 / INTERACTION PRINCIPLE
We're keeping one sentence at the top of Codex's design instructions:
FAMILIAR UX. UNFAMILIAR PRESENTATION.

That's our guardrail.
Navigation remains navigation.
Buttons behave like buttons.
Forms behave like forms.
Scrolling behaves like scrolling.
But their presentation can be memorable.
No scroll hijacking.
No mystery navigation.
No fake loading screen.
No forced "ENTER EXPERIENCE."
No cursor that makes using the website worse.
No animation hiding information from somebody trying to get to it.
12 / ACCESSIBILITY IS PART OF THE AESTHETIC
WCAG 2.2 AA remains our baseline.
But I don't want Codex thinking:
"Build the cool version and then make it accessible."

It's:
Build the cool version accessibly.

Focus states become part of our cobalt interaction language.
Reduced motion becomes part of our motion system.
Readable typography informs our composition.
Contrast informs the environmental transitions.
Keyboard behavior informs navigation.
Semantic HTML informs structure.
Accessibility isn't an appendix to Neo Miami.
It's part of Neo Miami.
